How the ARI platform works.

One members-only place where developers sell apartments and land plots before completion, and investors buy them early. ARI checks every project and every investor in between.

In short

Three parties, one clear process.

Developers list

Verified developers add apartments and land plots with a pre-sale discount.

ARI checks and matches

Experts review every listing. Investors see what fits their budget first.

Investors buy

Investors send interest. ARI arranges the viewing and supports the deal.

The full process

Nine steps across three roles.

Both sides register before they see listings or tools. The lock marks each registration step.

    Developer Platform Investor Registration gate
    Step by step

    What happens at each stage.

    Steps 1–3

    The developer lists

    1. 1
      Developer

      Creates an account

      The developer registers the company with its ID code. ARI verifies the company before the first listing goes live.

    2. 2
      Developer

    1. 4

      Investor

      Investor

    1. 7
      ARI

    3. 9
      Developer

      Receives the lead

      ARI confirms the investor’s budget, passes the lead to the developer and supports both sides until signing.

    Questions

    Frequently asked questions.

    Who can register?

    Real-estate developers with a registered company in Georgia, and investors from Georgia or abroad.

    Why are prices hidden until I register?

    Developers offer pre-sale discounts they don’t publish on open listing sites. Showing them only to registered investors protects their public price and keeps offers relevant.

    How is the discount calculated?

    The developer states the discount against their market price. ARI checks how it was calculated before the listing goes live.

    Does ARI guarantee the investment?

    No. ARI checks documents and supports the deal, but every purchase decision is the investor’s own. Each listing shows the facts you need to decide.

    What happens after I send interest?

    An ARI sales manager confirms your budget and contacts you to arrange a conversation or a site visit with the developer.

    Can investors from abroad use the platform?

    Yes. ARI works in Georgia and Cyprus and supports international investors throughout the process.

    Is registration free?

    Yes. Registration is free for developers and investors.
